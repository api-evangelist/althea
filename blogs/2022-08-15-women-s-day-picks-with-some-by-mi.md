With Women's Day just around the corner, we thought we would highlight products from Some By Mi , a female-owned brand that has become a fave of K-beauty lovers all around the world in the past few years. Owned by CEO Lee Ji Eun , Some By Mi was born from a rising trend and growing demand for natural, organic, and vegan beauty. The name of the brand comes from the words “something” and “by a miracle”, combined with “mi,” meaning “beauty” in Korean.
